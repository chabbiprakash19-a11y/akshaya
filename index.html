GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before your visit.

How soon will I get my test reports? +

Most reports are ready within 3 hours. Some specialised tests may take longer.

Do you offer home sample collection? +

Yes. A trained phlebotomist can collect samples at home across Akshaya Nagar and nearby areas.

Is Sri Spectra NABL accredited? +

Yes. Sri Spectra is NABL accredited and follows national quality and competence standards.

What are your timings? +

Monday to Saturday: 7 AM to 10 PM. Sunday: 7 AM to 2 PM and 5 PM to 10 PM.

How do I book a test? +

Call or WhatsApp 99866 77662, or use the appointment form on this website.
