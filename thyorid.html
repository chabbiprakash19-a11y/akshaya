THYROID HEALTH

Check your thyroid. Know your health.

Thyroid tests are blood tests used to assess how well the thyroid gland is functioning. Your doctor may recommend thyroid testing when you have symptoms or as part of a health evaluation.

At Sri Spectra Diagnostics, samples are collected carefully and processed using laboratory procedures designed to provide dependable results.

WHEN TESTING MAY BE CONSIDERED

Listen to what your body tells you.

Thyroid problems can sometimes cause changes in the way you feel. A healthcare professional can determine whether testing is appropriate.

✓

Unexplained changes in weight

✓

Unusual tiredness or fatigue

✓

Changes in heart rate

✓

Changes in mood or energy

✓

Changes in sensitivity to temperature

✓

Other symptoms requiring medical evaluation
