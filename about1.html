OUR STORY

A neighbourhood lab, grown with you.

Sri Spectra Diagnostics and Clinic has been part of the Akshaya Nagar community since 2014. What started as a neighbourhood laboratory has grown into a comprehensive diagnostic centre.

Today, we bring pathology, ultrasound, digital X-ray, ECG, health checkups and doctor consultations together under one roof.

Our approach is simple — provide dependable diagnostics, reduce waiting time and make healthcare convenient for families close to home.
